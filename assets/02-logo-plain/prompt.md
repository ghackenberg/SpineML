# Logo (Plain) Prompt
**Goal:** Combine the graphical icon with a large, balanced 'SpineML' wordmark.

**Reference Assets:**
- `assets/01-icon/icon.png` (Upload this to Gemini)

**Prompt:**
> "Combine this graphical icon with the full text 'SpineML' on a **SOLID WHITE BACKGROUND**. 
> 
> **Layout & Proportions:**
> 1. Place the icon to the left of the text.
> 2. **CRITICAL:** The 'SpineML' text must be large and prominent. The x-height of the lowercase letters should be at least 60% of the icon's height. 
> 3. Ensure a tight, professional tracking (letter spacing) and a balanced gap between the icon and the wordmark.
> 
> **Typography:** Use a bold, clean, modern, slightly technical sans-serif font.
> 
> **Style:** Professional engineering software brand. Ensure the color weights of the text and icon are perfectly matched."
