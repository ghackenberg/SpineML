# Social Preview Prompt
**Goal:** Create a cinematic hero image with a strict overhead-served spine architecture.

**Reference Assets:**
- `assets/03-logo-slogan/logo-slogan.png` (Upload this to Gemini)
- `screenshots/salabim.png` (Upload this as a style/layout reference)

**Prompt:**
> "Create a cinematic, 3D wide-angle visualization of an automated 'Spine Factory' layout. 
> 
> **STRICT SPATIAL ARCHITECTURE:**
> 1. **The Central Spine:** Exactly one central, straight red corridor axis. 
> 2. **Main-Spine Storages:** All storage units (large **orange end-storages** and large **red intermediate storages**) are located directly **ALONG the central spine**. 
> 3. **Side Corridors:** Production corridors branch off at 90-degree angles from the red intermediate storages. 
> 4. **Machines:** Industrial machines are placed along the side corridors.
> 
> **OVERHEAD SERVICE LOGIC:**
> 1. **Ceiling-Mounted Rails:** All robots and material flow paths are mounted on the **CEILING**, not the floor.
> 2. **Top-Down Service:** Both the main-spine robots and the side-corridor robots serve all storages and machines from the **TOP**. 
> 3. **Main Robot:** A sleek **red and green cube** on overhead rails travels along the spine, picking up and dropping material into the orange and red storage cubes from above.
> 4. **Side Robots:** Similar **red and green cubes** on overhead rails travel down side corridors, serving machines from above.
> 
> **VISUAL STYLE: AUGMENTED DIGITAL TWIN:**
> 1. **Nested Elements:** Every concrete 3D element (machines, robots) must be 'contained' within its corresponding abstract volume (colored cubes) from the simulation.
> 2. **Translucency:** Use translucent or wireframe abstract volumes to reveal detailed industrial components inside.
> 3. **Indicators:** Thin, glowing red and green laser-like rails represent the overhead travel paths.
> 
> **Composition:**
> 1. Place the 'logo-slogan.png' **PROMINENTLY IN THE CENTER** of the image.
> 2. The scene should be a high-tech, slightly atmospheric background for the logo.
> 
> **Format:** 1200x630 pixels, high-resolution hero image."
