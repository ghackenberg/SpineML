# Logo with Slogan Prompt
**Goal:** Add the technical slogan to the logo on a white background with vertical centering.

**Reference Assets:**
- `assets/02-logo-plain/logo.png` (Upload this to Gemini)

**Prompt:**
> "Take this logo and add the slogan 'The Python Toolbox for Spine-Based Manufacturing' on a **SOLID WHITE BACKGROUND**.
> 
> **Composition & Alignment:**
> 1. The slogan should be placed directly below the 'SpineML' wordmark, perfectly left-aligned with the start of the text.
> 2. **CRITICAL:** The entire text block (the wordmark 'SpineML' and the slogan together) must be **VERTICALLY CENTERED** relative to the graphical icon on the left.
> 3. Move the 'SpineML' textmark slightly upwards from its original position to make room for the slogan, ensuring the whole group is balanced.
> 
> **Typography:** Use a smaller, medium-weight version of the same sans-serif font for the slogan. The slogan should be clear and legible but secondary to the main project name.
> 
> **Balance:** Maintain the white background and ensure the entire composition feels like a cohesive, high-end industrial brand."
